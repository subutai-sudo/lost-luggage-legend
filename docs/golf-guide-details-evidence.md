# Course-specific golf guide evidence map

Checked: 2026-10-25. All 37 catalog records have dedicated golf prose, three playing notes, visitor access and three planning questions. Raw publisher excerpts remain in the local research archive, not this public repository or browser props.

## Source scope

- Course features and named-hole notes are paraphrased from the sources listed for each exact record. Planning questions are things to verify, not claims of available facilities or approved access.
- The final guide ledger cites 69 distinct body-source references, with 21 record-local source metadata records; additional monthly climate references resolve through the shared source catalog.
- No fee amounts, invented handicap cut-offs, minute-level transfer times or guaranteed playing conditions are published. Destination photographs remain explicitly labelled, not represented as course photographs.

## Independent-review corrections

- Bali National: the operator directory reports closure since May 2025 and a proposed February 2027 reopening. Both monthly notes now explicitly treat the former layout as historical/climate context, not a currently playable recommendation. The contradictory generic booking text is disclosed, and direct written confirmation is required.
- Brautarholt: the current official guide confirms 18 holes since July 2025, a weather-dependent 1 May–15 October season and special-request after-8pm access in the stated summer window. General national season advice no longer overrides the club calendar.
- Jack's Point: December discloses greens-aerification closures for 15–19 December 2026 and 14–19 December 2027 instead of a blanket peak-rate assertion.
- El Prat: the current course index and five-nine article take precedence over the inconsistent expat article for layout identity. Pink, Yellow and Green are full-length routing options; Pink and Yellow hole observations retain their exact original scope.
- Royal Melbourne: ordinary enquiries are limited to international/interstate golf-club members on Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays, excluding public holidays and subject to availability; reciprocal arrangements are separate.
- Private/restricted access is explicit. Unknown visitor eligibility remains unconfirmed rather than presumed public.
